# Code of Conduct

Genesis+ is built for beginners, so this project should be a place where people feel comfortable asking questions and making mistakes.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new to programming.
- Be respectful of differing opinions and experiences.
- Give and receive constructive feedback graciously; criticise ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask for clarification when something is unclear.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel run by the project) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers by email: [Jmcustoms1609@gmail.com](mailto:Jmcustoms1609@gmail.com).

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or other contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behaviour is harmful.
